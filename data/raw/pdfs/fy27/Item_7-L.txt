SECOND:                                                                        Res. No. 26-

RE:            AMEND THE FISCAL YEAR 2027 FISCAL PLAN TO BUDGET AND APPROPRIATE
               THE REMAINING BALANCE OF $1,142,840,551 FOR ESTIMATED ENCUMBERED
               PURCHASE ORDERS AND CONTRACTS, ESTIMATED UNENCUMBERED
               OPERATING INITATIVES, CAPITAL CONSTRUCTION PROJECT AND GRANT
               PROJECT BALANCES, ESTIMATED NON-CAPITAL SMALL PROJECT
               CONSTRUCTION BALANCES, ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT OPPORTUNITY FUND
               GRANTS, AND THE ESTIMATED UNENCUMBERED COMMUNITY SERVICES
               BALANCES SUPPORTED BY STATE AND FEDERAL REVENUE AS OF JUNE 30, 2026

ACTION:

              WHEREAS, purchase orders and contracts encumbered at the end of the fiscal year
must be re-appropriated in the succeeding fiscal year for payment; and

                WHEREAS, capital construction projects are seldom completed within one fiscal
year, which requires the budget and appropriation of any remaining balance of project funding
from one fiscal year to the succeeding fiscal year, to accomplish the project’s objective; and

                WHEREAS, the encumbrances are estimated at $439,131,619 as of
June 30, 2026; and

                 WHEREAS, appropriation of an estimated remaining balance amount of
$681,208,932 for unencumbered capital project and grant project balances, as well as the
remaining operating funds for information technology initiatives, including complex reporting and
performance management reporting tools, and case management and land records management
software will give the responsible agencies continuous access to funding; and

                 WHEREAS, appropriation of an estimated remaining balance amount of $4,500,000
for internal service fund carryover of expenditure balances in the capital project fund, general fund
and other funds will give the Public Works, Construction & Operations/Construction Crew
continuous access to project funding; and

               WHEREAS, appropriation of an estimated remaining balance amount of $2,000,000
for unencumbered Economic Development Opportunity Fund Grants will give the responsible
agencies continuous access to project funding; and

               WHEREAS, appropriation of an estimated remaining balance amount of
$16,000,000 for unencumbered Community Services balances supported by state and federal
revenue to ensure continuous operation of services provided by Community Services programs;
and

               WHEREAS, appropriation of an estimated remaining balance amount for the
beginning of the fiscal year facilitates the payment of outstanding bills and ensures continuity of
ongoing projects; and

               WHEREAS, a properly advertised public hearing was held on April 7, 2026;

               NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED that the Prince William Board of County
Supervisors hereby amends the fiscal year 2027 fiscal plan to budget and appropriate the
remaining balance of $1,142,840,551 for estimated encumbered purchase orders and contracts,
estimated unencumbered operating initiatives, capital construction project and grant project
balances, estimated non-capital small project construction balances, Economic Development
Opportunity Fund grants, and the estimated unencumbered Community Services balances
supported by state and federal revenue as of June 30, 2026;

                BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the Prince William Board of County Supervisors
hereby authorizes the Director of Finance to adjust this amount downward, if necessary, to
accurately reflect the actual encumbered amounts and actual unencumbered capital construction
project and grant project amounts at the end of Fiscal Year 2026.
