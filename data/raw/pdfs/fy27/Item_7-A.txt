RE:            ADOPT FISCAL YEAR 2027 REAL ESTATE PROPERTY TAX LEVY, FIRE LEVY,
               PERSONAL PROPERTY TAX LEVIES, BUSINESS, PROFESSIONAL AND

               PERSONAL PROPERTY TAX RELIEF, SPECIAL DISTRICT LEVIES, AND PARKS AND
               RECREATION FEES TO SUPPORT THE FISCAL YEAR 2027 ALL FUNDS BUDGET

ACTION:

               WHEREAS, the public hearing regarding the Fiscal Year 2027 real property tax levy
was duly advertised on March 26, 2026, and March 28, 2026, and held on April 7, 2026; and

               WHEREAS, the public hearings regarding the remaining Fiscal Year 2027 tax levies
were duly advertised on March 26, 2026, and March 28, 2026, and held on April 7, 2026, and the
public hearings regarding the Parks & Recreation fees were advertised on March 26, 2026, and
March 28, 2026, and held on April 7, 2026; and

              WHEREAS, the Prince William Board of County Supervisors on July 29, 2025, passed
Ord No. 25-43 which adopted the 3% food and beverage tax levy pursuant to VA Code §58.1-3833;
and

                WHEREAS, the Prince William Board of County Supervisors expressed interest in
amending the current food and beverage tax levy from 3% to 2%, effective January 1, 2027, that will
require a public hearing and amendment of the Prince William County Code of Ordinances;

                NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED that the Prince William Board of County
Supervisors hereby adopts the real estate tax rate of $0.850 per $100 of assessed value as the
Fiscal Year 2027 (Tax Year 2026) real property tax levy;

                BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the Prince William Board of County Supervisors
hereby directs the County Executive to prepare amendments to Chapter 26 of the Prince William
County Code of Ordinances, Article XX, titled Prince William County Food and Beverage Tax
Ordinance, pursuant to the Code of Virginia § 58.1-3833, to change the levy amount to a 2% food
and beverage tax in Fiscal Year 2027 effective January 1, 2027;

                BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that in compliance with Titles 58.1 and 27, VA Code
Ann., the Prince William Board of County Supervisors hereby adopts the fire levy rate of $0.070 per
$100 valuation for Fiscal Year 2027 (Tax Year 2026) on all real estate and restricts these funds for
fire and rescue purposes;

                BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the Prince William Board of County Supervisors
hereby adopts the following Fiscal Year 2027 (Tax Year 2026) special levies at a rate per $100
valuation on all real estate and restricts these funds for the special levy districts as follows:
