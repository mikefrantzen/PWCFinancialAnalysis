EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Fiscal Year 2027 (FY2027) budget adoption is scheduled for April 21, 2026.

During budget recap on April 7, 2026, the proposed budget was funded at a real estate tax rate of $0.886
per $100 of assessed value. The total FY2027 general revenue estimate increased $112,500 compared to
the proposed budget presented in February which was based on a real estate tax rate of $0.906. Of this
amount, the County government share of the revenue increase was $48,116 and the Schools’ share was
$64,384 based on the County/Schools revenue sharing agreement. Additional general fund budget recap
adjustments resulted in the proposed FY2027 budget having a remaining balance of $193,410.

The Prince William Board of County Supervisors (BOCS) marked up the proposed FY2027 budget on April
14, 2026. The following adjustments were made during the markup session:

FY2027 Budget Markup - Tax Rate and Revenue Changes:

   •   Real Estate Tax – The real estate tax rate was reduced from the budget recap rate of $0.886 to
       $0.850 per $100 of assessed value. The revenue impact is a $48,508,000 decrease in real estate tax
       revenue compared to budget recap.

   •   Business Tangible Personal Property Tax – Computer Equipment and Peripherals – The business
       tangible personal property tax rate on computer equipment and peripherals was increased from
       $4.15 to the advertised tax rate of $4.50 per $100 valuation. The revenue impact is a $21,700,000
       increase in personal property tax revenue compared to budget recap. During budget markup, the
       BOCS exempted the revenue generated by increasing the tax rate from $4.15 to $4.50 per $100
       valuation from revenue sharing with Prince William County Schools (PWCS). The entire $21,700,000
       revenue increase is a County government resource. This policy change is reflected in Item 7-C.

   •   Food and Beverage (Meals) Tax – The food and beverage tax rate was reduced from 3% to 2%. The
       revenue impact is a $6,000,000 decrease in general revenue based on an implementation date of
       January 1, 2027 (half-year impact in FY2027). The full-year revenue decrease in FY2028 is
       $12,400,000. Adjusting the food and beverage tax rate requires an amendment to the County

FY2027 Budget Markup – Changes to School Transfer:

   •   Transfer to Prince William County Schools (PWCS) – As shown in the table above, the County’s
       transfer to PWCS resulting from budget markup decreases $31,051,853 compared to budget recap.
       The Schools’ share of general revenue through budget markup is $1,087,989,229. This is a
       $31,051,853 decrease compared to budget recap but a $96,380,185 increase compared to the
       FY2026 adopted school transfer.

       As reflected in agenda item 7-D (Budget and Appropriate Fiscal Year 2027 School Budget), PWCS
       has allocated the change in County funding to their operating fund.

Budget Highlights in Budget Adoption Resolutions

   •   Tax Rates – The markup budget is funded at a real estate tax rate of $0.850 per $100 of assessed
       value in FY2027. The business tangible personal property tax rate on computer equipment and
       peripherals is $4.50 per $100 valuation. The business tangible personal property tax rate for the
       general classification is also $4.50 per $100 valuation. The personal property tax rate for vehicles is
       $3.50 per $100 valuation.

   •   Fire Levy Rate – Funding Fire and Rescue system-wide initiatives requires a levy rate of $0.070 per
       $100 of assessed value in FY2027.

   •   Mosquito and Forest Pest Management Levy – Funding the mosquito and forest pest management
       program in the Department of Public Works requires a levy rate of $0.0020 per $100 of assessed
       value in FY2027.

   •   Stormwater Management Fee – The stormwater management fee will be $67.28 for single family
       homes and $50.46 for townhouses, apartments, and condominiums.

   •   Solid Waste Fee – The solid waste fees will be $84.00 for single family homes, $75.60 for
       townhouses, $67.20 for mobile homes, and $56.20 for apartments and condominiums.

   •   Development Fees – Development fee schedules will increase as follows: Land Use Application
       (zoning, rezoning, and special use permit) fees will increase 15.0%, Land Development fees will
       increase 8.5%, Fire Marshal’s Office fees will increase 7.0%, and Building Development fees will
       increase 3.5%.
