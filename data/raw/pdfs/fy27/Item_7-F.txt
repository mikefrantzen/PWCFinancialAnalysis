SECOND:                                                                      Res. No. 26-

RE:            BUDGET, APPROPRIATE, AND TRANSFER FISCAL YEAR 2027 TRANSIT SPECIAL
               REVENUE FUND

ACTION:

               WHEREAS, the Potomac and Rappahannock Transportation Commission (PRTC) will
receive County transit support in the amount of $29,865,600; and

                WHEREAS, the above levels of County transit support for commuter bus and intra-
county bus will allow PRTC to continue providing County subsidized bus service, including
dedicated paratransit service and microtransit; and

                WHEREAS, funding PRTC operations requires revenue of $17,623,500 in Fiscal Year
2027 estimated fuel tax receipts, revenue of $5,900,000 in Fiscal Year 2027 grantor’s tax revenue,
and revenue of $3,450,000 in Fiscal Year 2027 transient occupancy tax (includes a $150,000
contribution for the Wheels-to-Wellness program); and

                 WHEREAS, a use of motor fuels tax fund balance totaling $1,670,756 will be needed
in Fiscal Year 2027; and

               WHEREAS, the public hearing regarding the Fiscal Year 2027 Budget was duly
advertised on March 26, 2026 and March 28, 2026 and held on April 7, 2026;

               NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED that the Prince William Board of County
Supervisors hereby budgets, appropriates, and transfers $17,623,500 from the County’s motor
fuels tax account at the Potomac and Rappahannock Transportation Commission for Fiscal Year
2027 operating and capital expenditures;

                BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the Prince William Board of County Supervisors
hereby authorizes $5,900,000 from the County’s grantor’s tax funds and $3,300,000 from the
County’s transient occupancy tax funds designated for transportation purposes, and $1,221,344
from Northern Virginia Transportation Authority 30% funds to support its transit obligations;

               BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the Prince William Board of County Supervisors
hereby authorizes $150,000 from the County’s transient occupancy tax funds be designated for
transportation purposes to support the Wheels-to-Wellness program.
