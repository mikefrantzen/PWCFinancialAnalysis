RE:            ESTABLISH BOARD OF COUNTY SUPERVISORS’ MAXIMUM ANNUAL
               COMPENSATION EFFECTIVE JANUARY 1, 2028

ACTION:

                WHEREAS, Virginia Code § 15.2-1414.2 states that prior to July 1 of each year the
current Prince William Board of County Supervisors (Board), by a recorded vote of a majority
present, shall set a maximum annual compensation, to be allowed each member which will
become effective as of January 1 of the year following the next regularly scheduled elections; that
any board may fix a higher salary for the Chair, or the Vice Chair, or both, than for the other
members of the Board; and that no increase in the salary of a member of the Board shall take
effect during the incumbent supervisor’s term in office; and

                WHEREAS, Prince William County’s compensation policy states that the County will
maintain salaries for its positions that are competitive with Fairfax, Loudoun, and Arlington
Counties, and the City of Alexandria and the Prince William Board of County Supervisors
implements this policy for County staff positions each year as part of the budget process; and

               WHEREAS, Board member compensation last changed in January 2024; and

               WHEREAS, County staff is recommending a 4.5% increase to Board of County
Supervisor salaries; and

              WHEREAS, Virginia Code § 15.2-1414.2 requires that any action to increase the
incoming Board compensation must occur prior to July 1, 2027, which is the election year for the
Board;

               NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED that the Prince William Board of County
Supervisors hereby establishes a maximum annual compensation for the members of the Prince
William Board of County Supervisors effective January 1, 2028, as follows:

       Chair At-Large                                              $88,552
       Magisterial District Supervisors                            $77,625
