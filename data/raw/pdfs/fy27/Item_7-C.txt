SECOND:                                                                          Res. No. 26-

RE:             ADOPT AND IMPLEMENT A NEW TAX POLICY FOR ADDITIONAL TANGIBLE
                PERSONAL PROPERTY TAX RATES ON PROGRAMMABLE COMPUTER EQUIPMENT
                AND PERIPHERALS EMPLOYED IN A TRADE OR BUSINESS AND COMPUTER
                EQUIPMENT AND PERIPHERALS USED IN A DATA CENTER IN RELATION TO THE
                REVENUE SHARING TAX POLICY WITH PRINCE WILLIAM COUNTY SCHOOLS

ACTION:

              WHEREAS, the Board of County Supervisors (Board) acknowledges there is a need to
implement a policy for dedicated funding for budgetary purposes; and

                 WHEREAS, the Board recognizes and acknowledges that it cannot fix the tax rate(s)
for future fiscal years at this time, nor can it budget and appropriate revenue for future fiscal years;
however, the Board can identify and recognize intended tax policies for future fiscal years and
provide direction to the County Executive regarding the preparation of proposed budgets in future
fiscal years; and

                 WHEREAS, the Board has previously adopted resolutions regarding tax policies,
specifically with regard to sharing revenue and providing additional funds to the Prince William
County Schools (Schools) where some, but not all, of the various revenues are shared with the
Schools, see Res. No. 98-1032, Res. No. 04-1034, Res. No. 13-257, Res. No. 15-292, and Res. No. 17-
18 ; and

               WHEREAS, pursuant to Sections 58.1-3506(a)(27) and 58.1-3506(a)(43), Virginia Code
Ann., the Board may adopt a business tangible property tax rate on programmable computer
equipment and peripherals employed in a trade or business and computer equipment and
peripherals used in a data center at a different rate from the tax levied on other tangible personal
property; and

                WHEREAS, the Board is interested in having a tax policy of increasing the business
tangible property tax on programmable computer equipment and peripherals employed in a trade
or business and computer equipment and peripherals used in a data center by 35 cents to fund
needs specific to Prince William County; and

               WHEREAS, the Board further recognizes and acknowledges that for each Fiscal Year,
any proposed tax rate increase will need to be properly advertised and a public hearing held
pursuant to Section 58.1-3007, Virginia Code Ann., before the increased rate is adopted;

                NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED that the Prince William Board of County
Supervisors hereby adopts and implements a new tax policy that beginning in Fiscal Year 2027 (Tax
Year 2026) the revenue generated from increasing the tangible personal property tax rates on
programmable computer equipment and peripherals employed in a trade or business and
computer equipment and peripherals used in a data center from $4.15 to $4.50 per $100 valuation
(or a $0.35 increase) will remain with the County and not be included in the revenue percentage split
contemplated by the County/Schools Revenue Sharing Agreement/Tax Policy;

               BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the Prince William Board of County Supervisors
directs the County Executive to include this tax policy in future proposed budgets until it is amended
by further Board action.
