RE:             AUTHORIZE AMENDMENTS TO THE POSITION CLASSIFICATION PLAN AND PAY
                PLAN IN ACCORDANCE WITH THE COUNTY’S ADOPTED COMPENSATION POLICY

ACTION:

              WHEREAS, Sections 19-12(c) and 19-13 of the Prince William County Code provides
for amendments to the Position Classification Plan and Pay Plan by resolution of the Prince William
Board of County Supervisors; and

                 WHEREAS, the Prince William Board of County Supervisors establishes Position
Classification Plans and Pay Plans. The County Executive delegates authority to the Human
Resources Director to maintain and administer the plans; and

                 WHEREAS, a classification and compensation study was conducted by the Human
Resources Department to ensure job classifications accurately reflect current roles,
responsibilities, and market conditions, and align employee duties with appropriate classifications,
ensuring internal equity, legal compliance, and improved recruitment/retention, while updating
outdated, inefficient job structures; and

               WHEREAS, the Human Resources Director recommends the Position Classification
Plan and Pay Plan amendments herein, as described below; and

                 WHEREAS, the Human Resources Director recommends the establishment of new
job classifications to accurately represent the evolving roles within the organization, to ensure
equitable compensation, and maintain a clear organizational structure, as described below; and

                   Establish Jail Lieutenant Colonel, AD08, (Regional Adult Detention Center)
                   Establish Jail Superintendent, E91, (Regional Adult Detention Center)

                 WHEREAS, the Human Resources Director recommends abolishing job
classifications to remove outdated or underutilized titles, obsolete roles, and aligning positions
with changing industry standards, as described below; and

                   Abolish Jail Superintendent, AD08, (Regional Adult Detention Center)

                WHEREAS, the Human Resources Director recommends updating pay scales and
salary structures to maintain a competitive, equitable, and legally compliant workplace, and
include adjusting to market fluctuations, retaining top talent, and ensuring internal equity, as
described below; and

                  Update the public safety pay scales for uniformed and sworn employees in
                   Adult Detention Center, Fire and Rescue, Police, and Sheriff’s Office at all levels
                   and pay scales
                  Update the General Services pay scale at the A01-A03 DBM level for minimum
                   wage changes from $12.77 per hour to $13.75 per hour effective January 1,
                   2027

              WHEREAS, an update to the Adult Detention Center and Sheriff’s pay scales for all
uniformed and sworn employees is recommended to receive an eight-point-two percent (8.2%)
market adjustment;

               NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED that the Prince William Board of County
Supervisors hereby amends the Position Classification Plans by approving the defined
amendments;

               BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the Prince William Board of County Supervisors
hereby amends the Adult Detention Center, Fire & Rescue, Police, and Sheriff’s Office pay scales by
approving the defined adjustments, effective July 1, 2026;

               BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the Prince William Board of County Supervisors
hereby amends the General Services pay scale at the A01-A03 DBM level by approving the defined
adjustments, effective July 1, 2026.
