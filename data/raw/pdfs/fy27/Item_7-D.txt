RE:             BUDGET AND APPROPRIATE THE FISCAL YEAR 2027 ALL FUNDS BUDGET AND
                ADOPT THE FISCAL YEAR 2027-2032 CAPITAL IMPROVEMENT PROGRAM

ACTION:

               WHEREAS, the public hearing regarding the Fiscal Year 2027 Budget and the Fiscal
Year 2027-2032 Capital Improvement Program (CIP) was duly advertised on March 26 and March
28, 2026, and held on April 7, 2026; and

               WHEREAS, the Prince William County Planning Commission conducted a duly
advertised public hearing on March 25, 2026, to solicit public comment on the Proposed CIP; and

               WHEREAS, the Prince William County Planning Commission recommended
adoption of the Proposed CIP as presented on March 25, 2026; and

                  WHEREAS, the Prince William County Board of County Supervisors’ budget and
appropriation of any federal, state, and/or County funds to the constitutional officers and their
offices, Prince William–Manassas Regional Adult Detention Center, Jail Board, Electoral Board,
Registrar, Office of Elections, Health Department, Juvenile Court Service Unit, Cooperative
Extension, and any other governmental office, agency, department, or entity which is exempt from
the County competitive service does not (i) reflect any intent by the Board or County to enter into a
joint-employment relationship regarding the employees of the aforementioned offices/entities; or
(ii) affect the exclusive authority of the aforementioned offices/entities regarding their employees,
including, but not limited to, the authority to hire, fire, terminate, dismiss, discipline, supervise,
manage, and/or approve or provide compensation, leave, and benefits to their employees;

               NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED that the Prince William Board of County
Supervisors hereby budgets and appropriates the Fiscal Year 2027 All Funds Budget of
$2,908,208,806 exclusive of School funds, in its entirety, effective July 1, 2026;

                BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the Prince William Board of County Supervisors
hereby adopts the Fiscal Year 2027-2032 CIP for County capital projects within the Capital Projects
Fund, the Solid Waste Capital Fund, and the Adult Detention Center Capital Fund to support the
projected expenditures for capital projects previously authorized by the Prince William Board of
County Supervisors;

                 BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the Prince William Board of County Supervisors
hereby determines that it is necessary and advisable to advance money on a temporary basis from

Greenway, Powells Creek Crossing, Fuller Heights Park Expansion, Neabsco District Park, and Solid

financings;

                  BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the Prince William Board of County Supervisors
hereby reasonably expects to use proceeds of authorized financings to reimburse cash advances
made or to be made to pay costs of the Juvenile Services Center, Homeless Navigation Center –
East, Homeless Navigation Center – West, Countywide Space, Woodbridge Library and Senior
Center, Fire and Rescue Station 27, Fire and Rescue Station 30, Fire and Rescue Station 3, Public
Safety Training Center Classroom, Judicial Center Expansion, Neabsco Greenway, Occoquan
Greenway, Powells Creek Crossing, Fuller Heights Park Expansion, Neabsco District Park, and Solid
Waste Facility Infrastructure projects that are paid prior to such issuances. The maximum amount
of debt or other financing expected to be issued for these purposes are: $60,000,000 for Juvenile
Services Center, $28,250,000 for Homeless Navigation Center – East, $8,150,000 for Homeless
Navigation Center – West, $4,000,000 for Woodbridge Library and Senior Center, $225,000,000 for
Countywide Space, $22,875,000 for Fire and Rescue Station 27, $35,000,000 for Fire and Rescue

Classroom, $200,000,000 for Judicial Center Expansion, $3,500,000 for Neabsco Greenway,
$4,658,000 for Occoquan Greenway, $9,500,000 for Powells Creek Crossing, $6,000,000 for Fuller
Heights Park Expansion, $6,000,000 for Neabsco District Park, and $22,410,000 for Solid Waste
Facility Infrastructure;

               BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the Prince William Board of County Supervisors
hereby intends that adoption of this resolution shall constitute “official intent” to reimburse the
expenditures with the proceeds of one or more issues of authorized financings within the meaning
of Treasury Regulations, Section 1.150-2, promulgated under the Internal Revenue Code of 1986,
as amended;

                 BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the Prince William Board of County Supervisors
hereby budgets and appropriates $213,758 in general obligation bond proceeds to the following
capital projects, Hellwig Park Artificial Turf, Howison Park Improvements, Neabsco Greenway,
Fuller Heights Park Expansion, Trails, Open Space and Access, and Ellicott Street (Occoquan
Greenway Connect), in Fiscal Year 2026 to support cost of issuance of the bond sale authorized by
Resolution 25-482 on September 23, 2025;

                BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the Prince William Board of County Supervisors
hereby authorizes a three percent merit pay adjustment for Constitutional Officers on July 1, 2026,

                BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the Prince William Board of County Supervisors
hereby authorizes the County Executive or designee to take such action as necessary with regard
to the Community Partner program, to include disbursement of the funds, to accomplish the intent

hereby authorizes the administrative adjustment of the adopted budgets, to include
interdepartmental transfer of budgeted salary lapse, to conform to defined program structures,
and to conform to internal service fund structures;

               BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the Prince William Board of County Supervisors
hereby authorizes the County Executive to shift vacant positions and vacant position budgets as
necessary to advance the goals and priorities of the Prince William Board of County Supervisors;

                BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the Prince William Board of County Supervisors

transition of Community Services to a dedicated fund to improve transparency and ensure more
