# Stage 8 — FY27 Adopted Budget: Data Extraction and Reconciliation

**Date:** 2026-04-24 (three days after adoption).
**Source:** Prince William County Board of Supervisors adopted the FY27 budget at its April 21, 2026 meeting via twelve resolutions (Items 7-A through 7-L) and an adoption memorandum. The proposed budget was transmitted February 18, 2026; budget markup April 14, 2026; public hearing April 7, 2026. All primary documents are archived at `data/raw/pdfs/fy27/`.

Purpose of this note: capture the FY27 figures the integrated model consumes, and record the reconciliation against the pre-adoption baseline used through Stages 1–7.

Two structural notes:
- The $0.35 C&P rate increment is **carved out of the 57.23% Schools revenue-share formula** (Item 7-C). The incremental revenue (~$21.7M at the adopted base) accrues 100% to the County.
- The meals-tax cut was introduced at markup on 2026-04-14 and takes effect mid-fiscal-year (2027-01-01); FY27 collections therefore reflect six months at 3% and six months at 2% (approximate half-year revenue impact −$6M; full-year FY28+ impact −$12.4M).

## 4. Schools transfer

- The 57.23% / 42.77% County–Schools revenue-sharing formula is retained (Item 7-C, Item 7-J).
- The formula base is amended: recordation (deed-of-conveyance) tax revenue and the $0.35 C&P rate increment are now excluded from the shared base and flow 100% to the County.
- FY27 adopted Schools share (of the 57.23% base): **$1,087,989,229** (adoption memo p.2, post-markup).
- FY27 total school-transfer package (including debt-service reimbursements, class-size-reduction grant, Gainesville HS DSE, cable franchise, security): proposed $1,122,631,736; adopted value is marginally lower.
- Year-over-year vs. FY26 adopted: +$96.38M, +9.7%.

## 6. Reserve and fund-balance policy

- Reserve-policy resolution (Item 7-I) maintained at prior levels.
- Data Center Revenue Stabilization Fund: **$0 FY27 contribution** (FY26 had $1,000,000 contribution; this line item was removed in the FY27 proposed budget, BudgetSummary line 429).
- Contingency reserve: $5,000,000 operating + $4,000,000 "Mitigate Potential State/Federal Impacts" (Introduction p.15) + $1,630,000 residual post-markup (adoption memo p.3).
- Capital Reserve use: $4,000,000 applied to public-safety school communications.
- Affordable Housing reserve: $5,000,000 one-time FY27 contribution.

## 7. Federal-contraction acknowledgments

The Proposed Budget Introduction (p.15) names state/federal uncertainty explicitly and earmarks $4.0M in contingency for federal-shutdown, UASI, Medicaid, and SNAP impacts. The acknowledgment is narrative rather than quantitative: no revenue line-item was reduced against a federal-contraction scenario in the adopted Five-Year Plan.

## 8. Digital Gateway / overlay references

Searched the twelve adoption resolutions, the adoption memorandum, the Proposed Budget Introduction, BudgetSummary, NonDept, DebtService, CIP, and Appendix sections for the strings "Digital Gateway," "Pageland," "overlay," "Oak Valley," and "rezoning." **No occurrences found.**

The Data Center Revenue Stabilization Fund line item — the reserve designated in prior budgets to absorb data-center-revenue shocks — received no new FY27 contribution. The $0.35 C&P rate increment, which produces ~$21.7M of predominantly data-center-sourced revenue, was ringfenced from the Schools formula by Item 7-C, but the adopting narrative does not frame the ringfence as a risk-management measure.
